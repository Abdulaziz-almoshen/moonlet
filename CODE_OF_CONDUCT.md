# Code of conduct

Moonlet is built by people with different backgrounds, languages, and levels of experience. We want every one of them to feel welcome.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Critique ideas and code, never people.
- Make room for newcomers; answer the question behind the question.
- Accept a "no" gracefully, and give reasons when you say one.

## What we don't accept

Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, personal attacks, publishing others' private information, and sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainers through the contact listed on the repository's profile, or with GitHub's reporting tools. Maintainers will review every report, keep it confidential, and may warn, temporarily ban, or permanently ban anyone who breaks this code, in any project space.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
